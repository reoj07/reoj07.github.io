JOER GALLEGO — DEVELOPER PORTFOLIO

QUICK START
1. Extract the ZIP archive.
2. Open index.html in Chrome/Edge, or place the complete folder in C:\xampp\htdocs\portfolio and visit http://localhost/portfolio/.
3. To publish on Hostinger/Plesk, upload the CONTENTS of this folder into your site's public_html folder (or your chosen subfolder). No database, build tools, or PHP are required.
